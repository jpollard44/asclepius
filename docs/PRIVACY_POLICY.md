## Who we are

## What we collect

- **Account**: your Apple ID subject identifier, and the name/email you chose
  to share via Sign in with Apple.
- **Health data you choose to sync**: daily aggregates from Apple Health
  (activity, heart, sleep, body measurements, workouts) and anything you log
  in the app (food, water, workouts, goals, measurements, chat messages with
  your coach).
- **Device data**: a push token if you enable notifications.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any
analytics that track you across apps.

## How we use it

- To show you your own data and progress.
- To power your AI coach: compact summaries of your data (never your raw
  Apple Health export) are sent to Anthropic's API to generate coaching
  responses. Food photos you submit are analyzed to estimate nutrition and
  are not stored.
- To send the reminders you turn on.

We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never use your
health data for anything except providing the service to you. This complies
with Apple's HealthKit guidelines.
